Our Journey

Our Story

Christ Covenant Deliverance Ministry (CCDM) Texas Worship Center was established an extension of a divine mandate that began in Nigeria to raise a praying church rooted in deliverance, revival, and scriptural truth. Inspired by the vision of our global founders, Venerable Tunde Bamigboye and Lady Evangelist Oriyomi Bamigboye, CCDM Texas serves as a spiritual hub for transformation, healing, and empowerment in the United States. Here, we uphold the “Pente-Orthodox” legacy a unique blend of apostolic depth, Pentecostal fire, and liturgical reverence while addressing the spiritual battles of our time through fasting, prayer, and the Word.

Humble Beginnings

From humble beginnings, we have grown into a vibrant community where chains are broken, lives are restored, and destinies are awakened. Through firebrand intercession, the ministry of the Word, and the prophetic move of the Holy Spirit, we remain committed to seeing God's people walk in total deliverance. We are more than a church we are a movement of liberation. Welcome to CCDM Texas Worship Center: where revival lives, and freedom reigns.

Guided Purpose

At CCDM Texas Worship Center, our purpose is clear: to serve God wholeheartedly and impact lives with intentionality, love, and power. United by unshakable faith and a calling to set the captives free, we pursue excellence in ministry and compassion in action. We guide souls to liberty through prayer, deliverance, and the unchanging Word of God. Every step we take is ordered by the Spirit as we labor not just for attendance but for transformation.

Today, we continue building upon our founders' vision while embracing innovation to meet contemporary needs, always remaining rooted in Scripture's timeless truths and God's unchanging love.

Mission & Vision

Our calling is clear: to lead souls to freedom through transformative deliverance, empowering vibrant faith in God’s kingdom.

Our Mission

To glorify God by delivering souls through fervent prayer, transformative deliverance, authentic worship, and biblical teaching, empowering believers to live free and impact their communities.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, multi-generational deliverance ministry where God’s mercy transforms lives, fostering faith, freedom, and purpose to shine His love worldwide.

Our Core Values

These values guide everything we do as a church community

Biblical Truth

We believe the Bible is God's inspired Word and the ultimate authority for faith and life, guiding all our teaching and decision-making.

Authentic Worship

We worship God with sincere hearts, expressing our love and gratitude through music, prayer, and lives surrendered to Him.

Community

We believe life is better together, fostering deep relationships where people can belong, grow, and support one another.

Compassionate Service

We serve others with Christ's love, meeting needs in our community and around the world with generosity and compassion.

Discipleship

We are committed to helping every person grow in their relationship with Jesus and become mature disciples who make disciples.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in all we do, honoring God with our best efforts while maintaining humility and grace.
